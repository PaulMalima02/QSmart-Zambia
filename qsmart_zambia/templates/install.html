Install QSMART on your phone

Works on Android and iPhone. No Play Store or App Store needed — install from the browser.

Important: Open this page on your phone (not a desktop). For the Install button to appear, the site must be served over HTTPS (or localhost). On the same Wi‑Fi use your PC’s IP, or a free tunnel like ngrok.

Android (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open QSMART in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm — QSMART appears on your home screen like any app.

If you see an Install banner at the bottom of the site, tap it.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open QSMART in Safari (not Chrome on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — the QSMART icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone does not show a browser “Install” popup; Share → Add to Home Screen is the official way.

After installing

  • Opens full-screen (no browser address bar).
  • Works offline for cached pages; live queues need internet.
  • Same login as the website — register once, use anywhere.
